Introduction

Three modest items, plenty of gold… and one slightly less modest item.

Kurosaï is a non-player character for Baldur’s Gate II: Enhanced Edition and EET. For a generous sum of gold, he combines three items of the same category and enchantment level into one with a higher enchantment.

He works with weapons, armor, shields, and jewelry, up to +6. Since version 3, he can also improve bracers of defense, down to Armor Class 0.

Some named items can be used as ingredients when they are equivalent to ordinary enchanted equipment. Kurosaï can combine standard items with the variants he recognizes.

Combining items and prices

Place the three ingredients in the party’s inventories and speak to Kurosaï. Recipes for which you have the required items and resources appear in the dialogue. The three ingredients are consumed, and the resulting item is given to the character speaking to him.

Three +X items → one +(X + 1) item

For example, three +3 medium shields can be combined into a +4 medium shield. The items must belong to the same category: bucklers, small shields, medium shields, and large shields each have their own recipes.

Enchanted equipment

The normal price is the desired enchantment squared × 1,000. With the gem option, it becomes the desired enchantment × 2,000, plus a gem.

A +4 medium shield therefore costs three +3 medium shields and 16,000 gp, or three +3 medium shields, 8,000 gp, and a star sapphire with the gem option.

Bracers of Defense

Bracers improve by Armor Class rather than a “+X” enchantment. You always need three pairs of bracers with the same AC to obtain one pair with an AC one point better.

Supported categories

FamilyCategories
Melee weaponsDaggers, short swords, long swords, scimitars, katanas, bastard swords, two-handed swords, battle axes, war hammers, clubs, maces, morning stars, flails, spears, halberds, and quarterstaffs.
Ranged weaponsLight and heavy crossbows; shortbows, longbows, and composite longbows; slings. Composite shortbows are supported with Rogue Rebalancing.
ArmorLeather armor, studded leather armor, chain mail, plate mail, and full plate mail.
ShieldsBucklers, small shields, medium shields, and large shields.
Jewelry and accessoriesRings, amulets, and cloaks of protection; bracers of defense.

Clubs have a special progression: their recipes start with three +3 clubs for one +4 club, from Throne of Bhaal onward.

Wakizashis, ninja-tōs, hide armor, and splint mail have no progression of their own: too few copies are available to collect three ingredients at each level.

Optional components

Recipes cost less but require rare gems

Replaces the normal prices with the gem prices shown above. This option is especially worthwhile at higher enchantment levels.

To find more gems, the mods Mersetek and Pym may help.

Kurosaï also appears in Amkethran

Kurosaï escapes Saradush and reappears in Amkethran. You can then continue combining items during Throne of Bhaal. With this option, obtaining certain +6 items becomes very easy.

Kurosaï also appears at the Nashkel Carnival — EET only

Adds Kurosaï to the Nashkel Carnival, so you can use his services as early as Baldur’s Gate I.

Items combined during BG1 can help you reach +6 in Throne of Bhaal if you keep your creations when moving to Shadows of Amn. EET Tweaks offers an option to import all party items from Siege of Dragonspear into Shadows of Amn.

Another tip: Thalantyr Item Upgrade lets you combine two +1 rings of protection into one +2 ring, making it easier to work toward a +6 ring.

An extra +1 item of each type in Shadows of Amn

Adds one extra +1 item of each supported type to various stores. Clubs are the exception: their recipes start at +3, in Throne of Bhaal.

This component also restores a Trademeet merchant who was fully coded in the game but left unused.

Compatibility and installation

Compatibility

This mod has no incompatibilities.

Installation and uninstallation

  1. Extract the Derats_Combino and Setup-Derats_Combino.exe into your game folder, alongside chitin.key.
  2. Run Setup-Derats_Combino.exe, choose your language, and install Kurosaï.
  3. Then install any optional components you want.

To change the installation or uninstall the mod, run the same program again.
